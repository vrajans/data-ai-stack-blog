The data and AI landscape moves fast. New table formats, catalogs, orchestration tools, vector databases and agent frameworks appear every month — and most content about them is either marketing, a shallow listicle, or a tutorial for one tool that skips the *why*.

This blog is my attempt at the opposite: **long-form, research-backed deep dives** that explain how the whole stack fits together, with honest trade-offs and practical decision frameworks.

## How the content is organized

Everything follows the flow of data, left to right:

1. **Data Architecture** — the blueprint: warehouses, lakes, lakehouses, table formats, catalogs, modeling, streaming, mesh and fabric.
2. **Data Engineering** — ingestion, change data capture, streaming, orchestration and transformation.
3. **Governance, Quality & Observability** — catalogs, lineage, contracts, access control and monitoring.
4. **Analytics & Semantic Layers** — turning data into trusted metrics and decisions.
5. **Machine Learning Systems** — features, training pipelines, MLOps and serving.
6. **Generative AI & Agents** — RAG, vector search, evaluation, LLMOps and agentic systems built on a solid data foundation.

Each track is published as a **series** you can read in order. You can see every planned part on the [series page](/series).
